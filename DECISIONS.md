# DECISIONS.md: Decision Log

Dated log of decisions that shape the project, with the reason and the alternatives.
What goes here: a choice someone could later question or want to reverse. What does not: routine changes (git history), orders (RULES.md), style habits (CONVENTIONS.md).

Order: newest last. Append only; never edit or delete an entry. To change a decision, add a new entry and mark the old one `superseded by <date / title>`.

## Entry format

### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short title
- **Decision:** what was decided.
- **Why:** the reason, and the constraint behind it.
- **Alternatives considered:** each option and why it lost.
- **Status:** active | superseded by <date / title>
