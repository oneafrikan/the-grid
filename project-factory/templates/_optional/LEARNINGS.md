# LEARNINGS.md: What Burned Us

Durable lessons: what went wrong or surprised us, so it is not repeated. One dated entry per lesson, with the cause and the rule it implies.
What goes here: a lesson someone paid for, and the rule that avoids paying again. What does not: a choice and its reasons (DECISIONS.md), a standing order (RULES.md), a log of events (git history, LOGS.md).

Order: newest last. Append only; never edit or delete an entry. If a lesson turns out wrong or no longer applies, add a new entry and mark the old one `superseded by <date / title>`.

## Entry format

### [YYYY-MM-DD] Short title
- **What happened:** the failure or surprise, in one or two lines.
- **Cause:** the root cause, not the symptom.
- **Rule:** what to do, or never do, because of it.
- **Evidence:** a commit, issue, doc or path where the detail lives.
